The dark streaks and green shadow creeping across your siding are not just dirt. They are living mildew and algae, feeding on the humidity that never really leaves South Louisiana. Blasting them off with high pressure can force water behind siding and damage the surface, so the right approach is a soft wash.

Soft washing uses low pressure plus a cleaning solution that kills the growth at the root and rinses it away, so your home comes clean and stays clean longer. Questions

Straight answers.

What is the difference between soft washing and pressure washing?+

Pressure washing uses high-pressure water and is right for hard surfaces like concrete. Soft washing uses low pressure plus a cleaning solution and is the safe choice for siding, stucco, and painted surfaces. We use whichever the surface calls for.

Will pressure washing damage my surfaces?+

No. We match the method to the surface. Concrete and other hard surfaces get pressure washed, while siding, stucco, and painted areas get a gentle low-pressure soft wash so nothing is harmed.
