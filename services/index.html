Driveway cleaning

Driveway cleaning

Your driveway is the biggest surface in front of your home. We bring it back to an even, clean finish with a flat surface cleaner, so there are no stripes. Most driveways take one to two hours and usually run about $100 to $250.

  • Surface cleaned edge to edge for an even finish
  • Oil spots and mildew treated
  • Joints and edges rinsed
  • Walkthrough with you when we finish

House soft wash

House soft wash

Green streaks and mildew make a house look older than it is. A soft wash uses low pressure and the right cleaner to lift it off without harming your siding. A single-story home usually runs about $250 to $500 and up.

  • Low pressure, safe for vinyl siding
  • Mildew, algae and dirt lifted off
  • Gutters, trim and foundation rinsed
  • Your home looks fresh again

Sidewalk and curb cleaning

Sidewalk and curb cleaning

Sidewalks, entry walks and the curb out front, brought back to clean concrete so the whole property looks sharp from the street. Sidewalks and walkways often run $75 to $200.

  • Front sidewalks, side walks and entry pads
  • Curbs cleaned along your frontage
  • Dirt and green growth lifted
  • Pairs well with a driveway cleaning
