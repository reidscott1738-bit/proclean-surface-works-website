The outside is part of the meal

In the restaurant business the exterior is part of the first impression, and it takes a beating. Foot traffic grinds gum and spills into the concrete. The sidewalk in front of the door collects grease tracked out from the kitchen and drink stains from to-go cups. Out back, the dumpster pad builds up grime that draws flies and smells, and drive-thru lanes collect oil and food waste.

A clean, bright entrance signals the same care customers hope to find in the kitchen. A dirty one makes people wonder.

Grease needs more than water

Grease and gum don’t come out of concrete with a rinse, even a hard one. Restaurant sidewalks and dumpster pads get a degreaser pretreatment that breaks down the oil, followed by hot commercial pressure washing to lift it out of the surface. Gum gets attention one spot at a time. Patios and walls get a softer touch: patio concrete is surface-cleaned evenly, and walls, signage and painted surfaces get a low-pressure soft wash.

Scheduled around service

You can’t close for cleaning, and nobody wants to step around a hose on the way in. We schedule early mornings before opening or on your off-days. Tell us your hours and delivery times and we’ll plan the visit so the sidewalk is clean and dry before the first customer shows up.

One-time deep clean or recurring

If the exterior has gone a long time without attention, start with a one-time deep clean to reset it. After that, a monthly or quarterly visit keeps the entrance and dumpster pad from building back up, and each visit is quicker and cheaper than another deep clean. We’ll recommend a schedule based on your traffic.

Part of our commercial work

Restaurant cleaning is part of our broader commercial pressure washing. If your restaurant shares a center with other businesses, ask about cleaning the shared sidewalk as well. And if you run more than one location, we can quote them together.
