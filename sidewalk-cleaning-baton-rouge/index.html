More than a looks problem

A dingy walkway makes the whole front of a property look neglected, but the bigger issue is footing. The algae and mildew that grow on shaded concrete form a thin, slick film, and after a Baton Rouge afternoon storm it can be as slippery as wet tile. That matters at home, with kids and older family members coming and going, and it matters even more at a business, where you are responsible for everyone walking up to your door.

Walkways also tend to be the first concrete to go dark. They sit next to flower beds and lawns that stay wet, under eaves that drip, and along fences and shrubs that block the sun.

How we do it

Flat runs of sidewalk get the same flat-surface cleaner we use on driveways. It keeps the spray at a steady distance and overlaps each pass, so the concrete comes out one even color instead of streaked. Steps, edges, the joints between slabs and the face of the curb get cleaned by hand with a wand. Then we rinse the mess off the concrete and away from the lawn edge so it looks finished.

Curbs and the street-side sidewalk

The curb and the public sidewalk in front of a house usually get ignored until the driveway is cleaned and they suddenly look twice as dirty next to it. They collect runoff from the street, lawn clippings and tire grime. If we’re already cleaning your driveway, adding the curb and street-side sidewalk is a small add-on that makes the whole frontage look finished. You can see the difference in the driveway-and-curb job on our before and after page.

Storefront sidewalks collect gum, drink spills and grease along with the usual mildew. We degrease and pressure wash entrances and frontage, and schedule around your hours so customers are never in the way. See commercial pressure washing and our page for restaurants.

How much does sidewalk cleaning cost?

Sidewalks and walkways often run $75 to $200 depending on length and buildup, and less per foot when bundled with a driveway. Every quote is free.

Do you clean the curb and the street-side sidewalk?

Yes. The curb and the public sidewalk in front of your home collect the same mildew and grime as your driveway, and we can clean them on the same visit.

Will cleaning make my walkway less slippery?

Removing the algae and mildew film is the main fix for a slick walkway. Clean concrete gives your shoes something to grip again, especially in shaded spots that stay damp.

Do you clean commercial sidewalks?

Yes. We clean storefront sidewalks and entrances for businesses, one time or on a recurring schedule, and can work early mornings or off-days.

How long does it take?

Most sidewalks and walkways take well under a couple of hours, and when they are bundled with a driveway the whole job usually fits in one to two hours.
