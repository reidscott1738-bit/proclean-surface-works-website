Questions

Straight answers.

Can you handle several properties?+

Yes. We work with property managers across multiple addresses, with clear per-property pricing and one point of contact. Recurring schedules are easy to set up.

Do you clean between tenants on short notice?+

We do our best to fit turnovers into the schedule. The more lead time you can give, the easier it is to hit your make-ready date.

Do you offer free quotes?+

Yes, always. Text a couple of photos of what you need cleaned and we can usually give you a price the same day, with no obligation to book.

Will pressure washing damage my surfaces?+

No. We match the method to the surface. Concrete and other hard surfaces get pressure washed, while siding, stucco, and painted areas get a gentle low-pressure soft wash so nothing is harmed.
