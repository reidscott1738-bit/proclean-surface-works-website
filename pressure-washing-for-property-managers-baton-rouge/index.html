Three problems, one crew

Managing rental property means juggling turnovers, curb appeal and liability, often across several addresses at once. Exterior cleaning touches all three.

Turnovers

Between tenants, a quick exterior clean makes a unit show better, rent faster and hold its price. Prospective tenants judge a property from the parking lot and the walk to the door, and a dark driveway or a mildewed breezeway tells them how the place is maintained.

Liability

Dirty walkways aren’t just unsightly. In the Baton Rouge climate, shaded concrete grows a slick film of algae, and a wet, algae-covered path or stair landing is a genuine slip hazard you’re responsible for. Keeping common walkways clean is one of the simplest risk reductions available.

Owner expectations

Owners notice when a property looks neglected. A regular cleaning schedule keeps exteriors in line with what owners expect without you having to chase it.

How we work with managers

  1. Send the list. Addresses, what needs cleaning at each, and any access notes about gates, pets or parking.
  2. Get per-property pricing. A clear number for each address so you can bill owners accurately.
  3. Schedule turnovers or a recurring plan. One-off make-readies, or a standing schedule for common areas.

Recurring service for common areas

For duplexes, fourplexes and small complexes, the common walkways, stairs and breezeways get dirty on a predictable cycle. Putting them on a quarterly or twice-a-year schedule keeps them consistently clean and safe and makes the cost easy to budget. Recurring visits are usually quicker and cheaper per visit than letting buildup get heavy.

Single-family rentals

For single-family rentals, the most common request is a turnover package: driveway, front walk, porch and a soft wash on the front of the house. It’s the same set of surfaces that matters for a home going on the market, because the goal is the same: make a good first impression on the next person through the door.

Questions from property managers

Still unsure? Call or text (225) 363-5220.

Can you handle several properties?

Yes. We work with property managers across multiple addresses, with clear per-property pricing and one point of contact. Recurring schedules are easy to set up.

Do you clean between tenants on short notice?

We do our best to fit turnovers into the schedule. The more lead time you can give, the easier it is to hit your make-ready date.

Do tenants need to be home?

Not necessarily. Tell us about access, gates, pets and parking at each property when you book so we can plan the visit.

Will pressure washing damage my surfaces?

No. We match the method to the surface. Concrete and other hard surfaces get pressure washed, while siding, stucco and painted areas get a gentle low-pressure soft wash so nothing is harmed.

Do you offer free quotes?

Yes, always. Text a couple of photos of what you need cleaned and we can usually give you a price the same day, with no obligation to book.
