A small river city south of Baton Rouge

Saint Gabriel is a small city on the east bank of the Mississippi River in Iberville Parish, just south of Baton Rouge along the Highway 30 and Highway 74 corridor. It incorporated in 1994 and blends longtime residential streets with the river’s industrial stretch.

Both of those mean plenty of humidity, dust and grime working on exterior surfaces. Concrete driveways and walkways darken with mildew, and siding picks up an algae shadow on the shaded side. It’s the same story as the rest of the river parishes, and a single cleaning resets it.

Older homes need the gentle approach

Older homes often have wood siding, painted trim and porch posts, and those surfaces don’t hold up to high pressure, which can strip paint, raise and splinter wood grain, and drive water into gaps. These surfaces get a soft wash: a cleaning solution that kills mildew and algae, time to work, then a gentle rinse. Concrete steps, walks and driveways can take real pressure and get the flat-surface cleaner for an even finish.

If you’re unsure which surfaces on your home need which treatment, that’s exactly what you’re hiring us to know. Our explainer on soft washing vs pressure washing walks through it.

Front porches and entries

Covered porches and front entries are where buildup shows first on a lot of river-parish homes. They stay shaded and damp, they collect tracked-in dirt, and mildew settles into the concrete. The covered entry in the photo above is a good example of how much one cleaning changes the first thing guests see.

What a visit looks like

Most Saint Gabriel customers have us clean a few things at once: a soft wash on the house, the driveway and walkways with a flat-surface cleaner, and the porch or entry. Most driveways and sidewalks take one to two hours, and a full house wash usually takes a few hours, with a time window given up front when we quote it. Combining surfaces into one trip lowers the price per surface because we only set up once. Typical ranges are a standard driveway at about $100 to $250 and a single-story house soft wash at about $250 to $500 and up; the cost guide explains what moves the number. Before we arrive, our prep checklist covers what to move and close.

Do you serve Saint Gabriel and nearby areas?

Yes. We cover Saint Gabriel and the surrounding east-bank river communities, and we are close by in Baton Rouge and Ascension Parish.

How much does pressure washing cost in Saint Gabriel?

A standard driveway usually runs about $100 to $250, sidewalks $75 to $200, and a single-story house soft wash about $250 to $500 and up. Every quote is free and up front.

Can you clean an older home without damaging it?

Yes. Older siding, wood trim and painted surfaces get a low-pressure soft wash with a cleaning solution instead of high pressure, which can strip paint and gouge wood.

Will pressure washing damage my surfaces?

No. We match the method to the surface. Concrete gets pressure washed, while siding, stucco and painted areas get a gentle low-pressure soft wash.
