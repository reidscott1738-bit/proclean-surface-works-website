The first impression nobody budgets for

A grimy sidewalk, a mildew-streaked entrance or a blackened dumpster pad quietly tells people you don’t sweat the details. Clean, bright concrete and a fresh building exterior do the opposite, and keeping them that way costs far less than most owners assume.

It’s also a safety issue. Algae on a shaded walkway and grease near a door make for slick footing, and at a business you’re responsible for everyone walking up to it.

The right method for each surface

Concrete gets commercial pressure and a flat-surface cleaner for an even finish. High-traffic areas get a degreaser pretreatment first, because grease, gum and drink stains don’t come out with water alone. Building walls, stucco, painted surfaces and signage get a low-pressure soft wash so nothing is etched, stripped or forced behind the facade.

Recurring cleaning

The cheapest clean is the one that happens before buildup sets in. Many businesses put their storefront and sidewalks on a monthly or quarterly schedule so the property always looks sharp without anyone having to remember. Recurring service is priced per visit and usually costs less each time than a one-time deep clean.
