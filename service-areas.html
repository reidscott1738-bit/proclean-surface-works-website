East Baton Rouge Parish

Baton Rouge is home base, from Spanish Town and Mid City to the Garden District, Southdowns, Shenandoah and the Highland Road corridor, where old live oaks keep concrete shaded and damp. North of the city, Zachary mixes newer subdivisions off Highways 19 and 64 with tree-lined older streets, and Central has larger lots and long driveways along Hooper, Sullivan and Wax Road near the Comite and Amite rivers.

Ascension Parish

Prairieville is full of newer homes off Highways 42, 73 and 44 that still pick up dark driveway edges within a few years. Gonzales sits at the I-10 and Airline Highway crossroads, with plenty of retail and restaurant frontage alongside its neighborhoods. Down the river, Geismar homes deal with the extra dust and grime of the industrial corridor.

Livingston Parish

Just across the Amite River, Denham Springs has lots of brick homes and the Antique Village downtown, and was hit hard in the 2016 flood. Walker, a little farther east on I-12, has grown fast with new subdivisions off Highway 447 and Walker South Road. We also cover nearby communities like Watson.

Iberville Parish

Saint Gabriel, on the east bank of the Mississippi south of Baton Rouge, rounds out our river-parish route along with Geismar and Gonzales.
