Your home is probably your biggest investment, and the Louisiana climate works against it every single day. Heat and humidity grow mildew on concrete and siding, spring pollen coats everything, and the shaded side of the house slowly turns green. Regular pressure washing keeps that buildup from becoming permanent staining and keeps your home looking like one that is well cared for.

Pro Clean Surface Works handles the full outside of your property. We pressure wash hard surfaces like driveways, patios, and walkways, and we soft wash the more delicate areas like siding and brick at low pressure so nothing gets damaged. One visit, and the whole place looks years younger.

Questions

Straight answers.

How much does pressure washing cost?+

Most residential jobs are priced by the surface and its size. Driveways and sidewalks often start around $100 to $200, and full house washing runs more depending on square footage and stories. Every quote is free and up front with no hidden fees. Call or text (225) 302-9540.

Will pressure washing damage my surfaces?+

No. We match the method to the surface. Concrete and other hard surfaces get pressure washed, while siding, stucco, and painted areas get a gentle low-pressure soft wash so nothing is harmed.

Do you offer free quotes?+

Yes, always. Text a couple of photos of what you need cleaned and we can usually give you a price the same day, with no obligation to book.

What all can you clean at my house?+

Driveways, sidewalks, patios, pool decks, porches, fences, and home exteriors. If it is outside and it is dirty, ask us and we will tell you the best way to handle it.
