Pro Clean Surface Works ("we," "us") respects your privacy. This policy explains what we collect when you use procleansurfaces.com and how we use it.

Information we collect

We only collect the information you choose to give us, such as your name, phone number, email, address, and details about the job when you call, text, email, or submit our quote form. We do not sell or rent your information to anyone.

How we use it

We use your information solely to respond to your request, provide a quote, schedule and perform work, and follow up about your service. If you contact us, we may keep a record of that correspondence.

Booking and forms

Our online booking is handled by a third-party scheduling provider, and any information you enter there is subject to that provider's own privacy policy. Our quote form sends your details directly to our business email.

Cookies & analytics

This site may use basic, privacy-respecting analytics to understand general traffic. We do not use your data for advertising profiles.
