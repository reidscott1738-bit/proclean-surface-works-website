How to keep garbage cans from smelling in the Louisiana heat

A trash can in a South Louisiana summer is basically a warm, damp box of bacteria. Here’s why it smells, how to clean it, and the habits that keep it from getting that bad again.

Why cans smell worse here

Every bag that goes into a garbage can leaves a little behind: a drip from a leaking bag, a few food scraps, liquid that pools in the bottom. In a cool, dry climate that residue dries out and mostly just sits there. In Baton Rouge, where summer days are hot and humid and a dark plastic cart can sit in full afternoon sun, the inside of the can gets very warm and stays damp. That’s ideal for bacteria breaking down food, and that’s where the smell comes from.

It also explains the flies. Strong odor draws them, and if residue sits long enough, maggots follow. A garden hose rinse moves some of the mess around but rarely gets the film off the walls and out of the bottom corners.

Habits that keep a can fresh

  1. Bag everything, and tie it tight. Loose scraps and leaking bags are the main source of residue.
  2. Double-bag the wet stuff. Seafood shells, crawfish boil leftovers, meat scraps and anything drippy should be double-bagged and, if possible, put out close to pickup day.
  3. Keep the can in the shade. A can out of the afternoon sun stays cooler inside and smells less.
  4. Air it out after pickup. On a dry day, leave the lid open for an hour after the truck comes so moisture can escape.
  5. Use an odor absorber. A layer of baking soda in the bottom of a clean, dry can soaks up odor and moisture.
  6. Don’t let liquid pool. If something spills, deal with it before it sits in the heat for a week.

How to clean a garbage can yourself

  1. Do it right after pickup, when the can is empty.
  2. Pick a spot on grass or near a drain where dirty water won’t sit on your driveway.
  3. Rinse out loose debris with a hose on a strong setting.
  4. Scrub with hot, soapy water and a long-handled brush. Pay attention to the bottom corners and the underside of the lid.
  5. Rinse thoroughly.
  6. Dry it in the sun with the lid open, upside down if you can.
  7. Add baking soda to the bottom once it’s dry.

Wear gloves and eye protection. If you use any cleaning product beyond dish soap, follow the label and never mix chemicals.

Why does my trash can smell so bad in summer?

Heat speeds up the bacteria breaking down food residue and liquids in the bottom of the can. A dark plastic cart sitting in the sun gets very hot inside, which makes odor stronger and draws flies.

How often should I clean my garbage can?

Whenever it starts to smell or you see residue in the bottom. In a Louisiana summer that can be every month or two, and less often in cooler months.

Does baking soda help a smelly trash can?

Yes. A layer of baking soda in the bottom of a clean, dry can absorbs odor and some moisture. It works best after the can has been washed.

How do I get rid of maggots in a trash can?

Empty the can, wash it out thoroughly with hot soapy water, dry it in the sun and keep food waste tightly bagged. Getting the residue out is what stops them from coming back.
