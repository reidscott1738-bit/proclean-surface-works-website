Questions

Straight answers.

Do you offer recurring cleaning for HOAs?+

Yes. Many associations set up quarterly or annual cleanings for entrances, sidewalks, and amenity areas so everything stays consistent. We'll recommend a schedule and give you one clear price to take to the board.

Can you give the board an itemized quote?+

Yes. We provide a clear, up-front quote you can present for approval, and we can break it out by area (entrance, sidewalks, amenity center) if that helps.

Will pressure washing damage my surfaces?+

No. We match the method to the surface. Concrete and other hard surfaces get pressure washed, while siding, stucco, and painted areas get a gentle low-pressure soft wash so nothing is harmed.

Do you offer free quotes?+

Yes, always. Text a couple of photos of what you need cleaned and we can usually give you a price the same day, with no obligation to book.
