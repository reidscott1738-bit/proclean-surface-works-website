Soft washing vs pressure washing

The difference in one sentence

Pressure washing blasts away grime with high-pressure water and is right for hard surfaces like concrete; soft washing uses low pressure plus a cleaning solution to safely kill and remove mold and algae from delicate surfaces like siding and roofs.

Using the wrong one is how surfaces get damaged, so matching the method to the surface is the whole job.

When pressure washing is right

Concrete driveways, sidewalks, patios, and pool decks can take high pressure, and a flat-surface cleaner leaves them evenly clean with no streaking. Brick pavers and most hardscape are also good candidates. These surfaces are durable and don't hold cleaning solution the way porous siding does.

When soft washing is the safe choice

Vinyl and painted siding, stucco, wood, and especially roofs should never be hit with high pressure, which can force water behind panels, strip paint, or tear shingles. Instead, soft washing applies a mildewcide solution at low pressure that kills the mold, mildew, and algae at the root, then rinses clean.

Because it kills the growth rather than just blasting the surface layer, a soft wash also stays clean noticeably longer than a high-pressure rinse.

How we decide on your home

We look at each surface and use whatever it calls for, often both on the same visit: high pressure on the driveway and walkways, a gentle soft wash on the siding and eaves. You don't have to know the difference; that's what you're hiring us for. Tell us what's dirty and we'll handle it the right way.

Questions

Straight answers.

Is soft washing safe for my siding?+

Yes. Soft washing is specifically the safe method for siding, stucco, and painted surfaces because it uses low pressure and a cleaning solution instead of force. It won't damage the surface the way high pressure can.

Which do I need for my roof?+

A roof should always be soft washed, never pressure washed. High pressure damages shingles. A low-pressure treatment kills the black algae streaks safely.

Can you do both on one visit?+

Yes, and we usually do, pressure washing the concrete and soft washing the house exterior in the same appointment.
