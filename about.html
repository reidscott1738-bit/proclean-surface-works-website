Who We Are

A local crew that treats your property like our own.

Pro Clean Surface Works is a locally owned, family-run pressure washing company based right here in Baton Rouge. We're a young, hard-working crew, and that's the feedback we hear most from customers: professional, and we hustle. We started this because we saw how many homes and businesses around the capital region were letting Louisiana's mold and grime win, and we knew we could fix it fast and do it right.

We're not a franchise or a call center. When you reach out, you're talking to the people who will actually be on your property, and we take that personally. Every job gets the right method for the surface, a clean-up afterward, and an honest answer about what we can and can't get out.
