Our story

Three friends from Baton Rouge

Pro Clean Surface Works was started by Grayson Allain, Riley Allain and Reid Scott, three friends from Baton Rouge who all grew up together at Parkview Baptist. We created this company because we wanted a simple, honest way to serve our community and take pride in the work we do.

A big part of what pushed us to actually start the business was the influence of people we look up to. One of our good friends, Andrew Lee, who owns Pro Ground Land Management, showed us what it looks like to build something from the ground up by taking action and making the most of the opportunities in front of you. Another major influence was our mentor, Coach Devin Ducote, whose mindset, discipline and encouragement played a huge role in shaping how we approach work and life.

We started Pro Clean Surface Works not just to clean driveways and sidewalks, but to take real steps toward our long-term goal of financial freedom. Like Andrew always says, it’s about taking action: not waiting around, not overthinking, but moving forward and building something real.

What began as an idea between brothers and a close friend turned into a way for us to serve the community we grew up in, learn as we go, and create opportunities for our future. It’s simple work, but it means something to us, and we’re proud of that.
