Our story

Pro Clean Surface Works was started by Grayson Allain, Riley Allain and Reid Scott, three friends from Baton Rouge who all grew up together at Parkview Baptist. Grayson and Riley are brothers; Reid has been their close friend for years. We created this company because we wanted a simple, honest way to serve our community and take pride in the work we do.

A big part of what pushed us to actually start the business was the influence of people we look up to. One of our good friends, Andrew Lee, who owns Pro Ground Land Management, showed us what it looks like to build something from the ground up by taking action and making the most of the opportunities in front of you. Another major influence was our mentor, Coach Devin Ducote, whose mindset, discipline and encouragement played a huge role in shaping how we approach work and life.

We started Pro Clean Surface Works not just to clean driveways and sidewalks, but to take real steps toward our long-term goal of financial freedom. Like Andrew always says, it’s about “taking action”: not waiting around, not overthinking, but moving forward and building something real.

What began as an idea between brothers and a close friend turned into a way for us to serve the community we grew up in, learn as we go, and create opportunities for our future. It’s simple work, but it means something to us, and we’re proud of that.

What you can count on

We show up

On time, ready to work, when we say we will. Your time matters.

We do it right

The correct pressure and solution for each surface, so nothing gets damaged. High pressure and a flat-surface cleaner for concrete; a low-pressure soft wash for siding, brick and stucco.

We keep it simple

Text a photo, get a price, pick a time. One clear quote up front, and an honest word if a stain is likely to fade rather than disappear.

We clean up

The only thing we leave behind is a property that looks taken care of.

What customers say about us

Our customers keep using the same words: hard-working and professional. Phillip Smith wrote that he was “pleased with the professionalism and hustle,” and Pam Baker called us “hard working and professional.” Read their full reviews on our reviews page.

What we do

We clean the hard surfaces around homes and businesses: driveways and concrete, house exteriors, sidewalks and curbs, patios and pool decks, garbage cans and commercial property. You can see real before and after photos from our jobs on our work page.

Where we work

We’re based in Baton Rouge and serve East Baton Rouge, Ascension, Livingston and Iberville parishes, including Prairieville, Gonzales, Denham Springs, Walker, Zachary, Central, Geismar and Saint Gabriel. See all of our service areas.
