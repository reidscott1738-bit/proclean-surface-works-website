A crossroads town that takes a beating

Gonzales, the self-proclaimed Jambalaya Capital of the World, sits at one of the busiest crossroads in Ascension Parish, where Interstate 10 meets Airline Highway and Highway 30. It’s a growing mix of established neighborhoods, new subdivisions and a long stretch of retail and commercial property around the highways, the outlets and the Lamar-Dixon Expo Center.

All of it faces the same Gulf Coast humidity that grows mildew on concrete and algae on siding. Gonzales adds a little extra: road grime and dust from heavy traffic on I-10 and Airline, and the airborne grit that comes with being near the river’s industrial corridor. That film settles on damp driveways, windowsills and storefront glass and makes surfaces look dull faster than they would farther from the highway.
