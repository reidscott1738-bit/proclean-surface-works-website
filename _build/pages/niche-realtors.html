Buyers decide in seconds

Most buyers form an opinion about a house before they open the front door, often from the main listing photo or the moment they pull into the driveway. A dark, mildew-stained driveway and a dingy exterior make even a well-kept home look tired and quietly suggest deferred maintenance. A freshly washed driveway, bright walkway and clean siding photograph beautifully and signal a home that’s been cared for.

In South Louisiana, where concrete and siding go dark faster than almost anywhere, the gap between “as is” and “cleaned” is usually dramatic. Compare the driveway above. It’s one of the cheapest, highest-return things a seller can do before listing.

What to clean before you list

Focus on what shows up in the photos and what buyers see first:

  1. Driveway and apron. It takes up a big part of the front exterior photo.
  2. Front walk, steps and porch. The path buyers walk to the door during a showing.
  3. Front of the house. A soft wash on the siding or brick removes the green shadow and gray film.
  4. Back patio and pool deck. If outdoor living is in the listing photos, it needs to look usable.
  5. Curb and street-side sidewalk. Small, but it finishes the frontage.

Timing it right

The ideal window is a few days before listing photos. Clean concrete needs a little time to dry completely so it shows its true color in pictures, and you want the exterior done before staging and landscaping touch-ups, not after. If a showing is already scheduled, call or text as early as you can and we’ll do our best to fit you in before it.
