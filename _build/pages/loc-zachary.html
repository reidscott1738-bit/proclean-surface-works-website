A family town at the top of the parish

Zachary sits at the north end of East Baton Rouge Parish, a family-focused community known for one of the top-rated public school systems in Louisiana. That reputation keeps demand for homes high, and it shows in the neighborhoods: newer developments off Highway 19 and Highway 64 have brought a wave of brick homes with wide concrete driveways, while the older streets near downtown have mature trees and established yards.

Both kinds of neighborhoods need cleaning, just for different reasons.

Older streets: shade and mature trees

The big trees that make Zachary’s older neighborhoods so pleasant also drop shade, pollen, leaves and sap all year. Shade is exactly what lets mildew and algae take hold on north-facing siding and concrete. The green shadow on the shady side of the house creeps a little wider every humid summer, and driveways under the canopy turn dark and streaky. Leaf tannins can leave brown outlines on concrete as well.

Newer subdivisions: bright concrete that doesn’t stay bright

In newer developments the concrete starts out light and even, which makes every bit of buildup show. Lawn edges, sprinkler overspray and the apron where cars turn in usually darken first. Light-colored siding and painted trim show streaks sooner than older, darker exteriors. A driveway that looked bright when the house was built can look tired in a few years. A single visit resets it.

Timing it around family life

A lot of Zachary families schedule a cleaning ahead of something: a graduation party, a crawfish boil, the holidays, or the in-laws coming to town. Late spring after the pollen falls is a popular window, and so is fall. If you have a date in mind, tell us when you reach out and we’ll plan around it. How we clean

Concrete driveways, patios and sidewalks get commercial pressure and a flat-surface cleaner for an even finish. Brick, siding and stucco get a low-pressure soft wash so nothing is damaged, and because it kills the growth instead of just rinsing it, the house stays clean longer.
