Buyers decide in seconds

Most buyers form an opinion about a house before they open the front door, often from the main listing photo or the moment they pull into the driveway. A dark, mildew-stained driveway and a dingy exterior make even a well-kept home look tired and quietly suggest deferred maintenance. A freshly washed driveway, bright walkway and clean siding photograph beautifully and signal a home that’s been cared for.

In South Louisiana, where concrete and siding go dark faster than almost anywhere, the gap between “as is” and “cleaned” is usually dramatic. Compare the driveway above. It’s one of the cheapest, highest-return things a seller can do before listing.

What to clean before you list

Focus on what shows up in the photos and what buyers see first:

  1. Driveway and apron. It takes up a big part of the front exterior photo.
  2. Front walk, steps and porch. The path buyers walk to the door during a showing.
  3. Front of the house. A soft wash on the siding or brick removes the green shadow and gray film.
  4. Back patio and pool deck. If outdoor living is in the listing photos, it needs to look usable.
  5. Curb and street-side sidewalk. Small, but it finishes the frontage.

Timing it right

The ideal window is a few days before listing photos. Clean concrete needs a little time to dry completely so it shows its true color in pictures, and you want the exterior done before staging and landscaping touch-ups, not after. If a showing is already scheduled, call or text as early as you can and we’ll do our best to fit you in before it.

For agents

If you list homes regularly, it helps to have someone you can call on short notice. Send us the address, the photo date and what needs cleaning, and we’ll send one clear price you can pass to your seller. Vacant listings and out-of-town sellers are fine as long as we can reach the areas being cleaned.

For sellers doing it yourself

If you’re selling without an agent, the same advice applies: the exterior photo does a lot of the selling. If you want to know what moves the price of a cleaning, our cost guide lays it out. And our guide on how to prepare for pressure washing covers what to move and close up before we arrive.

Questions

Questions from agents and sellers

Still unsure? Call or text (225) 363-5220.

Can you clean before my listing photos?

Yes. Give us your photo or open-house date and we will schedule around it. A clean exterior makes a real difference in how a listing photographs and shows.

How fast can you get out for a listing?

We move quickly for sellers on a deadline. Call or text as early as you can and we will do our best to fit you in before your photos or showing.

Can the seller be out of town?

Yes. As long as we can reach the areas being cleaned, the seller or agent does not need to be there.

What should be cleaned before listing?

The driveway, front walk, porch and the front of the house matter most because they show up in the main listing photo and are the first thing buyers see. The back patio is next if it is in the photos.

How much does pre-listing cleaning cost?

It depends on what is included. A standard driveway usually runs about $100 to $250 and a single-story house soft wash about $250 to $500 and up. A whole-home package costs less per surface than separate visits.
