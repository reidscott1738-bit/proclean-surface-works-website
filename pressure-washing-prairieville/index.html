Serving Prairieville

Louisiana weather is hard on your surfaces. We fix that.

Prairieville has grown fast, and a lot of its homes and driveways are only a few years old. That does not make them immune to Louisiana's climate. Tucked between Baton Rouge and Gonzales in Ascension Parish, Prairieville gets the same heat, humidity, and pollen that grows mildew and algae on concrete and siding, and shaded lots in the newer subdivisions off Highway 42 and Highway 73 hold moisture even longer.

Even a five-year-old driveway can pick up dark streaks and a green tint along the edges before an owner realizes it. Soft-washed siding and a cleaned driveway make a newer home look sharp again, which matters in neighborhoods where homes turn over and curb appeal counts.

Most residential jobs are priced by the surface and its size. Driveways and sidewalks often start around $100 to $200, and full house washing runs more depending on square footage and stories. Every quote is free and up front with no hidden fees. Call or text (225) 302-9540.

Do you serve all of Prairieville?+

Yes, all of Prairieville and the surrounding Ascension Parish neighborhoods, including the subdivisions off Highway 42, Highway 44, and Airline Highway.

Will pressure washing damage my surfaces?+

No. We match the method to the surface. Concrete and other hard surfaces get pressure washed, while siding, stucco, and painted areas get a gentle low-pressure soft wash so nothing is harmed.
