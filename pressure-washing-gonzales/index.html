A crossroads town that takes a beating

Gonzales, the self-proclaimed Jambalaya Capital of the World, sits at one of the busiest crossroads in Ascension Parish, where Interstate 10 meets Airline Highway and Highway 30. It’s a growing mix of established neighborhoods, new subdivisions and a long stretch of retail and commercial property around the highways, the outlets and the Lamar-Dixon Expo Center.

All of it faces the same Gulf Coast humidity that grows mildew on concrete and algae on siding. Gonzales adds a little extra: road grime and dust from heavy traffic on I-10 and Airline, and the airborne grit that comes with being near the river’s industrial corridor. That film settles on damp driveways, windowsills and storefront glass and makes surfaces look dull faster than they would farther from the highway.

For Gonzales homeowners

For a homeowner, the difference between a driveway that looks cared for and one that looks tired is usually just a cleaning. We see the same patterns across town: concrete darkened along the lawn edges and under trees, gray siding on the shaded side of the house, and front walks that get slick after rain. A single visit resets all of it.

  • Driveway and concrete cleaning with an even, stripe-free finish
  • House soft washing for siding, brick and stucco
  • Sidewalks, entries and curbs
  • Patios, porches and pool decks
  • Garbage can cleaning, added to any visit

For Gonzales businesses

With so much retail and restaurant traffic along Airline Highway and Highway 30, a business’s exterior is part of how customers judge it. A grimy sidewalk or a mildew-streaked storefront quietly costs you walk-ins. We clean storefronts, sidewalks, entrances, dumpster pads, drive-thru lanes and building exteriors, and we schedule early mornings, evenings or off-days so cleaning never gets in the way of business.

Do you clean commercial property in Gonzales?

Yes. We clean storefronts, sidewalks, entrances, dumpster pads and building exteriors for Gonzales businesses, including recurring cleanings on a schedule that suits you.

Why does my Gonzales home get dirty so fast?

Along with the usual heat, humidity and pollen, homes near I-10, Airline Highway and the industrial corridor pick up extra road grime and airborne dust that settles on damp surfaces.

How much does pressure washing cost in Gonzales?

A standard driveway usually runs about $100 to $250, sidewalks $75 to $200 and a single-story house soft wash about $250 to $500 and up. Commercial work is quoted per property. Quotes are free.

Can you clean before an event or the holidays?

Yes. Give us a date and we will schedule the work ahead of it. Booking a week or two out gives the most flexibility.

Will pressure washing damage my surfaces?

No. We match the method to the surface. Concrete gets pressure washed, while siding, stucco and painted areas get a gentle low-pressure soft wash.
