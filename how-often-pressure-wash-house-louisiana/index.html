How often should you pressure wash your house?

The simple schedule for South Louisiana

In most of the country the answer is every couple of years. In South Louisiana it's more often, because our heat and humidity grow mold, mildew, and algae faster than almost anywhere. As a rule of thumb: soft wash the house exterior about once a year, and clean concrete driveways and sidewalks once or twice a year.

North-facing walls and anything under shade stay damp longer and grow the green film faster, so they often need attention sooner than the sunny sides.

Why Louisiana is different

Our long, humid summers, frequent rain, and heavy spring pollen create a near-perfect environment for organic growth on exterior surfaces. Add the pollen that coats everything each spring and the storm debris from hurricane season, and surfaces here simply get dirty faster than in a drier climate. Staying on a regular schedule keeps buildup from becoming permanent staining.

Signs it's time

You don't have to track dates, your house will tell you. Look for: green or black streaks creeping across siding; a driveway that's noticeably darker than it used to be; slippery, discolored walkways; black streaks on the roof; or a general dinginess you stopped noticing until a neighbor got theirs cleaned. Any of these means it's time.

Regular cleaning saves money

Mold and algae don't just look bad; they hold moisture against your siding, roof, and concrete and shorten their life. Regular, gentle cleaning is far cheaper than repainting, re-roofing, or replacing concrete, which is why an annual wash is one of the smartest small investments in a home down here.

About once a year for a soft wash of the siding is right for most Louisiana homes, though shaded, north-facing walls may need it sooner.
