Questions

How much does pressure washing cost in Baton Rouge?

Most residential jobs are priced by the surface and its size. A standard driveway usually runs about $100 to $250, sidewalks and walkways often $75 to $200, and a single-story house soft wash about $250 to $500 and up. Every quote is free and given up front, with no surprise fees.

Do you offer free quotes?

Yes. Every quote is free and there is no obligation. Text a couple of photos of what you need cleaned and we can often price it the same day.

Will pressure washing damage my concrete or siding?

No. We match the method to the surface. Concrete driveways and sidewalks get pressure washed with commercial equipment and a flat-surface cleaner, while siding, stucco and painted areas get a low-pressure soft wash so nothing is damaged.

How long does a typical job take?

Most driveways and sidewalks take one to two hours. A full house wash usually takes a few hours. We give you a time window up front when we quote the job.

What areas do you serve?

We are based in Baton Rouge and serve East Baton Rouge, Ascension, Livingston and Iberville parishes, including Prairieville, Gonzales, Denham Springs, Walker, Zachary, Central, Geismar and Saint Gabriel.

Can I get several surfaces cleaned in one visit?

Yes, and it usually lowers the price per surface. Most customers bundle the driveway, walkways and house wash, and you can ask to add garbage can cleaning to the same trip.
