Good to know

Still unsure? Call or text (225) 363-5220.

How much does it cost?

Every property is different, so we start with a free quote. As a ballpark, a standard driveway usually runs about $100 to $250, sidewalks and walkways $75 to $200, and a single-story house soft wash about $250 to $500 and up.

What do you clean?

Driveways, house siding with a soft wash, sidewalks and curbs, patios and pool decks, garbage cans and commercial property. Have something else in mind? Ask in the quote form.

Will it damage my property?

We remove dirt, algae and buildup without damaging your property, and we match our approach to the surface: high pressure and a flat surface cleaner for concrete, a low pressure soft wash for siding, brick and stucco.

What happens after I ask?

We reach out within a day, set a time, do the job, then walk it with you so you see the result before we pack up.

What areas do you serve?

We are based in Baton Rouge and serve East Baton Rouge, Ascension, Livingston and Iberville parishes, including Prairieville, Gonzales, Denham Springs, Walker, Zachary, Central, Geismar and Saint Gabriel.

How long does a job take?

Most driveways and sidewalks take one to two hours. A full house wash usually takes a few hours. We give you a time window up front when we quote the job.
