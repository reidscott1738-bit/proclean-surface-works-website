How much does pressure washing cost in Baton Rouge?+

Most residential jobs are priced by the surface and size. Driveways and sidewalks typically start around $100 to $200, and full house washing usually runs more depending on square footage and stories. Every quote is free and given up front, with no surprise fees. Call or text (225) 302-9540 for a fast quote.

Do you offer free quotes?+

Yes. Every quote is free and there's no obligation. Text a couple of photos of what you need cleaned and we can often price it the same day.

What areas do you serve?+

We're based in Baton Rouge and serve the surrounding area, including Prairieville, Gonzales, Denham Springs, Geismar, Saint Gabriel, and the rest of East Baton Rouge, Ascension, and Livingston parishes.

Will pressure washing damage my concrete or siding?+

No. We match the method to the surface. Hard surfaces like concrete driveways and sidewalks get pressure washed with commercial equipment, while more delicate surfaces like siding, stucco, and painted areas get a low-pressure soft wash so nothing is damaged.

How long does a typical job take?+

Most driveways and sidewalks take one to two hours. A full house wash usually takes a few hours. We give you a time window up front when we quote the job.
