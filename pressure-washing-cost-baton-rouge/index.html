The short answer

Most residential pressure washing jobs in the Baton Rouge area fall somewhere between about $100 and $600, depending on what's being cleaned and how big and dirty it is. Small jobs like a standard driveway or a set of sidewalks often start around $100 to $200, while a full house wash usually runs more, roughly $250 to $500 and up, depending on square footage and the number of stories.

Those are honest ballpark ranges, not a quote. The only way to know your real price is a free estimate, and because we can price most jobs from a couple of photos, it takes about a day to find out. Call or text (225) 302-9540 and we'll give you a number with no obligation.

What actually drives the price

Size of the area. Pricing tracks square footage closely. A one-car driveway is a fraction of a long double driveway with a parking apron and walkways.

The surface and method. Flat concrete cleaned with a surface cleaner is efficient. Delicate surfaces like siding, stucco, or a roof need a slower, low-pressure soft wash with a cleaning solution, which takes more time and product.

How bad the buildup is. A driveway cleaned every year cleans up fast. One that hasn't been touched in a decade, with heavy mold, red clay, or deep oil staining, takes more work and sometimes pretreatment.

Access and setup. Easy water access and open areas are quick. Tight gates, second-story reach, or long hose runs add time.

Add-ons. Bundling the driveway, house wash, and walkways in one visit is usually cheaper per surface than booking them separately.

Why quotes vary so much between companies

Some of the low online prices you'll see are for a bare-minimum rinse, not a proper clean with the right pressure and solution for each surface. Others are inflated to leave room to negotiate. A fair quote is specific to your property and explained up front, with no surprise fees added at the end.

As a local, family-run crew, we price honestly and tell you what's realistic before we start, including whether a stain will fully come out or just improve.

Is pressure washing worth it?

For most homeowners, yes. A wash is a small fraction of the cost of repainting or replacing concrete or siding, and it removes the mold and algae that actually degrade those surfaces over time. Before selling a home, it's one of the highest-return things you can do for curb appeal.
