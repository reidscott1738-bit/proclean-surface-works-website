Terms of service

The basics of working with us and using this site. Last updated: October 2026.

These terms apply to your use of procleansurfaces.com and to services provided by Pro Clean Surface Works.

Quotes and estimates

Quotes are free and are estimates based on the information and photos you provide. Final pricing may be confirmed on site if conditions differ from what was described. We’ll always talk it through with you before starting.

Our work

We match the cleaning method to each surface to protect your property. Some stains, especially old oil, rust or deep organic staining, may improve rather than disappear completely; we’ll tell you honestly what to expect before we begin.

Scheduling

Weather can affect outdoor cleaning. If we need to reschedule for safety or quality, we’ll contact you to arrange a new time.

Website content

Information on this site is provided in good faith for general guidance. Photos of work and reviews are genuine. Price ranges are general guidance, not quotes. We may update content and pricing guidance at any time.

Contact
